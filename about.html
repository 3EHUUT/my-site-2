About Golden Hour Beauty

We started Golden Hour Beauty in 2026 with a simple idea: finding great beauty products shouldn't feel overwhelming. With thousands of serums, palettes, and skincare lines launching every month, it's easy to get lost in the noise. We're here to cut through it.

What We Do

Our small team of beauty editors and skincare enthusiasts spends hours each week testing, researching, and comparing products so you don't have to. We look at ingredients, value for money, real user experiences, and long-term results — not just hype. Every product we recommend has earned its spot.

Whether you're building your first skincare routine or searching for that perfect everyday lipstick, we curate honest picks at fair prices. No filler, no fluff — just products we'd genuinely recommend to a friend.

Why Trust Us

  • Our editorial team brings over a decade of combined experience in beauty, skincare science, and cosmetics writing
  • We only feature products we've thoroughly vetted — paid sponsorships never influence our picks
  • We update our recommendations regularly as formulas change and better options hit the market

How We Keep the Lights On

We earn a small commission through affiliate links, which means when you purchase a product through our site, we may receive a small percentage at no extra cost to you. This is what allows us to keep doing what we love — finding the best beauty products and sharing them with you.
