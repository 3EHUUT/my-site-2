How to Build Your Perfect Routine

Expert tips to help you shop smarter, not harder

1

Start with Skin Type, Not Hype

The most viral product might not suit your skin. Identify whether you're oily, dry, combination, or sensitive before investing. CeraVe and The Ordinary are great starting points because they label formulations clearly.

2

Layer Products Thinnest to Thickest

Apply serums before creams, and always finish with SPF in the morning. This ensures each product absorbs properly instead of sitting on top of the previous layer.

3

Invest Where It Counts

Spend more on items that stay on your skin (serums, moisturizers, sunscreen) and save on rinse-off products like cleansers. A $12 cleanser and a $60 serum is a smarter split than the reverse.

4

Give Products Four to Six Weeks

Skin cell turnover takes roughly a month. Switching products every week means you never see real results. Commit to a routine and track changes with weekly photos.

5

Heat Tools: Quality Over Quantity

One great styling tool with adjustable heat settings will protect your hair far better than three cheap ones. Look for ionic technology and multiple temperature controls.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often

How do I know which moisturizer is right for my skin type?
If your skin feels tight after cleansing, go for a rich cream like CeraVe or Tatcha Dewy Skin Cream. If you get oily by midday, a lightweight gel moisturizer is better. Combination skin? Use a lighter formula on your T-zone and a richer one on your cheeks.
Is the Dyson Airwrap really worth the price?
For most people who style their hair regularly, yes. It consolidates multiple tools into one, uses less direct heat than traditional irons, and lasts for years. If you only air-dry your hair, you probably don't need it.
What order should I apply my skincare products?
Cleanser → toner (optional) → serum (like The Ordinary Niacinamide) → moisturizer → sunscreen in the morning. At night, swap sunscreen for a heavier night cream or treatment like Olaplex No. 3 for hair.
